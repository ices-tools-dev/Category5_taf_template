# Category5_taf_template

TAF template for the Category 5 method.

The example is the sandeel of area 6 (san.sa.6), assessed in 2019, with catches by half year from the HAWG sandeel assessment data.

- `data.R`: the catches of area 6 by half year, from `bootstrap/initial/data/sandeel_assessment_1982_2018.csv`.
- `output.R`: the SAG upload file, `output/sag_upload.xml`, made with icesSAG, and its inputs as tables.
- `report.R`: the tables (`report_tables.R`), the plots (`report_plots.R`) and a Word report made from `report.Rmd` (`report_doc.R`).

Copied from [ices-taf-dev/ices_cat_5_template](https://github.com/ices-taf-dev/ices_cat_5_template) at commit 64a6ff6.
